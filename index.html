Add it to Gmail

  1. In Gmail, click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new.
  3. Name it, click in the box, and paste (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V).
  4. Scroll down and click Save changes.
Headshots from other sites may not show in this preview. Open the link in a browser tab to check it loads; it will appear once pasted into Gmail.
